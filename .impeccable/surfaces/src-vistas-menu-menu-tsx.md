# Main menu — Operate
Latest direction: dark cognitive aesthetic for older adults. Compact brain heading, no filters, distinct pastel game accents on dark neutral panels. Keep the five calendar routes and menu-only navbar. Remove duplicated invitation, empty categories, catalog count, promotional footer and guest points. Nunito Sans and large touch targets preserve reading and ease of use. Mobile cards use full-width descriptions and compact 116x48px play buttons. Each miniature plays a brief game-specific demonstration once 65% visible and re-arms after fully leaving view; reduced motion uses gentle color/opacity feedback without movement.

Navigation uses AppNavbar with menu/calendar/game contexts and centralized session logic. Dark navbar styling applies to menu, calendar and game contexts. All consumers use AppNavbar directly; it owns the hint button and animated points. King’s Court is excluded from this design stage. DESIGN.md is the menu-based design authority.
